## What Collector's Edition Is

Collector's Edition is an iOS app for tracking personal collections (trading cards, comics, LEGO sets, figures, and more). It lets you catalog items, store photos, record purchase information, and view statistics about your collection. It is not a marketplace, auction service, or valuation authority. Any prices or values shown are informational only.
